The Path of Dharma

The Rāmāyaṇa, Rāma’s journey, was sung by Maharṣi Vālmīki, the first poet. It tells how Lord Viṣṇu, born as Rāma, prince of Ayodhyā, walked the earth as a man and showed what a son, brother, husband, friend and king can be.

The Text

Śrīmad Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, the Ādi Kāvya of Maharṣi Vālmīki. Devanagari quotations are from the original verses.

valmikiramayan.net

Sarga numbering follows valmikiramayan.net, with verse translations by Desiraju Hanumanta Rao and K. M. K. Murthy. Every passage links to its sarga there.

This Retelling

Written afresh as one flowing kathā, following Vālmīki together with the beloved tradition of the Rāmcaritmānas and the Kamba Rāmāyaṇam, so the episodes every household knows are told as they are told in temples and homes.
